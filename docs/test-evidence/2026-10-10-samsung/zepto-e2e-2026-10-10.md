# Controlled Samsung Zepto product-to-cart verification — 10 October 2026

## Scope and consent

The user authorized an existing saved Zepto delivery location, a single reversible test-item add, cart review and removal. No checkout, payment, purchase, account change, private entry, complaint, certificate bypass or model call was permitted or performed.

The device was a Samsung SM-M076B on Android 16. This document intentionally omits the delivery address, device identifier, raw hierarchy and screenshots. Temporary captures and the raw device XML were deleted after visual verification.

## Evidence

1. On the public milk-results surface, Saathi was asked for an exact visible product. It produced marked-Add guidance for that same public product, with a fresh snapshot in 246 ms and zero model calls.
2. After one user-authorized Add, Saathi observed the exact product's compact quantity state and the one visible Cart action. Its fresh snapshot completed in 353 ms and it instructed the user to review quantity and cart choices without claiming an order.
3. Opening Cart showed the same single test item. The item was removed immediately. The results screen returned to its marked-Add state, and a second no-tap observation again produced the exact product's Add guidance.
4. The focused physical observation test passed for both the added/cart and cleared/Add states. The existing four-test Samsung `CommerceGuidanceTest` regression suite passed after its temporary overlay-window read race was corrected. The full Android local unit suite passed with 166 tests, and debug/test APK builds succeeded.

## Boundaries

This is evidence for one current public Zepto screen and one product-to-cart review path. It does not certify arbitrary items, substitutions, availability, saved locations, checkout, payment, purchases, all Zepto versions, other retailers, continuous voice, OEM background behavior or universal Android accessibility compatibility. Saathi did not tap Zepto, type any value, make a payment decision, choose a substitute or call a model during the observations.
